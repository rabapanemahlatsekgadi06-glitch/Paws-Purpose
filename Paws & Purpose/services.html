How Adoption Works

Our adoption process is designed to make sure every animal goes to a safe, suitable home:

  1. Browse our available animals below or vist the shelter in person.
  2. Submit an adoption enquire using our enquiry form.
  3. Meet the animal and chat with our team about your home and lifestyle.
  4. Complete a short adoption agreement and pay a small adoption fee. It covers vaccination and sterilisation costs.
  5. Take your new companion home😊.
