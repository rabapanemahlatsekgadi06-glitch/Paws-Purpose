This respitory contains POE. The project is a website built for Paws & Purpose, a local non-profit animal
shelter. 

Website Goals and Objectives
1) Increase adoption enquires submitted through the site.
2) Grow the shelter's volunteer base.
3) Increase once-off and recurring sponsorships.
4) Number of adoptions, volunteers or sponsor form submissions per month and page visits to the "Adopt a Pet" section.

Key Features and Functionality
1) Homepage with hero introduction and featured adoptable animals.
2) About Us page detailing the shelter's history, mission , vision and team.
3) Adopt a Pet page listing available animals and the adoption process.
4) Enquiry page with a form for adoption, volunteering or sponsorship enquires.
5) Contact page listing two physical locations and a general contact form.
